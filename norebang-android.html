Karaoke Number Search: TJ & KY Privacy Policy

App data

Search queries and favorites are handled on your device. The app connects to GitHub to check for and download song database updates. It does not send your queries or favorites to that update service. The app does not require an account. You can clear app data in Android settings.

Banner ads and data sharing

Google AdMob can collect and share approximate location derived from your IP address, app interactions, diagnostics and crash information, and advertising and other device identifiers. Google uses these data to deliver and measure advertising, analyze performance, and prevent fraud. The SDK encrypts these data in transit.

Where the app displays Privacy choices, you can change your choices there. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See how Google uses data from partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy.
